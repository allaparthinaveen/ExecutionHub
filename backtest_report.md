## 💡 Key Strategy Takeaways
* **Asymmetric Risk profile**: Equal-weight allocation across screen passes guarantees that a single 10x-50x winner (e.g. `NPST.NS` generating 5,000%) completely offsets all losing drawdowns (capped at -100%) and drives overall portfolio outperformance.
* **Cash Flow Quality and Promoters Pledging Check**: The combination of operating cash flows and promoter safety rules ensures that paper earnings and heavily leveraged companies are screened out, maintaining high asset quality.
